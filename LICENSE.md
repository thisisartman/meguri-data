## Catalogue

The catalogue files (`JP-<code>-<type>.csv.gz`) list publicly announced facts: which cards, stamps and tags exist, where they are handed out, and when. They were compiled from the public pages of prefectures and cities, the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (dam cards), JR East and the GKP manhole card listings. Each row's `source_url` names its source.

Pictures of cards and stamps are not included here. They belong to their owners.
